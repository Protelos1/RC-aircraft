**Today i worked on some essential pieces of design for the LiteSky airframe\!**  
I have created  
1)A battery holder to stop it moving around and affecting weight in flight  
2\) A wing strut that will go between the pieces of depron foam to give it support and also that Clark Y airfoil shape  
3\) A internal bulkhead this strengthens the inside of the cabin for landings and help everything stay connected.  
---

Design bits:  
You will notice how the wing strut and the bulkhead have cutouts in them this is to reduce the weight as much as possible. I had to be careful that it did not weaken the integrity of the pieces so that they can complete what they are designed for.  
---

The battery tray holder was especially designed for reusable cable ties to thread through and under the battery holder.  
You may have to use 2 ties depending on battery size.  
But the holder has a specific under channel to allow the tie to sit flush with the plastic and being allowed to be glued down inside the aircraft.

Rc plane \- Final design\!\!\!\!  
Woah that took a while lol.  
**You can scroll through the images to see it in progress and then the finished model.**  
I have finished iteration 1 of the final design for the Rc plane\!  
The only things i expect to change now to this model is maybe a couple of diameters but apart from that i think all of the major work for the design is done.  
These are the things i ‘researched’ and considered during the design process  
1  
The wing type  
For a rc plane lift is important as well as simplicity when making this it is hard to make wing designs that fold and bend this is why i have chosen the **’Clark Y** aerofoil design. This design is used on GA (general aviation \- small planes like cessna e.c.t) and is also extremely common in other RC planes.  
2  
I have also considered the **DIMENSIONS** for this aircraft  
The wing is 50cm long and 10cm wide and in person it will be 1.2 cm thick (at the thickest point)  
The horizontal stabilizer (the mini wing at the back)  
Is 7.5 cm on each side so 15cm in total  
The cabin/fuselage is 37.5 cm **75%** of the wingspan  
**And out all important target weight is under 250g**  
3  
WING DESIGN  
It is a high wing design for stability in the air and is normally considered the easiest design to fly.  
It is 12% of chord which is 1.2cm max thickness at the biggest point  
The Peak of the thickness will be 3cm behind the leading edge (front of the wing)  
4  
**THE CABIN**  
The cabin is designed to be 4cm along and 5cm height to allow for LiPo batteries receivers and ESC (Speed controller \- flight computer)  
5  
**LANDING GEAR**  
The landing gear is in a traditional tail dragger design with a rear wheel  
The stance of the gear will be at a forward and downward angle so the wheels sit slightly ahead of the wings leading edge to prevent flipping over also to absorb max impact without breaking  
As well as the gear i am going to test belly landings (landings with no gear) they have to be preformed on something soft grass for example. But because the plane is so light belly landings should not be an issue.
